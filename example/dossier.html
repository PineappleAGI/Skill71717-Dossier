      Research Question Framework

      Lens used to structure this literature scan — not a finding of the papers themselves.

      Core inquiry intent

      Comparative / Benchmarking

      Primary lens: is ChatGPT systematically better than Claude on capability, human preference, and safety? Secondary: when ‘better’ is task-specific or reversed.

      Epistemic rigor

      Frontier, weakly controlled

      A handful of peer-reviewed domain bake-offs (radiology, dentistry, breast-cancer scenarios) sit beside vendor cards, a live preference leaderboard, and many off-topic high-citation surveys.

      Scope boundary

      Chat assistants, 2023–2026

      NLP / LLM evaluation. In: papers or pages that mention ChatGPT/GPT-4-class and/or Claude, or that define how those two are compared. Out: O-RAN, robotics world models, driving theses, and generic surveys with no bake-off.

      What's missing from this list

      • Almost no harvested journal paper runs a matched, current ChatGPT (GPT-4o / o-series) versus current Claude (Sonnet / Opus) protocol on the same items. The live Arena board and vendor blogs carry that comparison more than journals.
      • Safety, cost, latency, tool-use, and long-context recall are thin; ‘better’ in this scan mostly means exam scores or human preference.
      • Thesis retrieval drifted into driving, construction, dermatology, and food safety — those records were dropped as off-topic.
      • arXiv returned HTTP 429 during harvest, so recent preprints (including some bake-offs) are under-sampled.
